Why this is happening

It is not the insulation they object to. It is not being able to see the roof.

Spray foam is applied directly to the underside of the roof. Once it is on, a surveyor cannot inspect the timbers beneath it — and a lender will not lend against a roof nobody can see.

What usually triggers the call

Four versions of the same problem

  • A mortgage or remortgage refused after the valuation came back
  • Equity release held up — often stricter than a standard mortgage
  • A sale falling through when the buyer's survey flagged the loft
  • A purchase you want to go ahead with on a house that already has foam
What actually resolves it

Removal, plus the paperwork

  • The foam removed back to clean, visible timber
  • The roof structure exposed so it can be inspected again
  • The loft reinsulated to a conventional specification
  • Documentation and certification to hand to the lender, the surveyor or the buyer

Straight answers

What homeowners ask us

Why do I need to remove my spray foam insulation?

In most cases because a lender, surveyor or equity release provider has raised it. Foam applied to the underside of the roof stops anyone inspecting the timbers, and lenders are generally unwilling to lend against a roof structure that cannot be seen. Removing it makes the roof inspectable again.

Will my roof need replacing afterwards?

Usually not. The point of removing it carefully is that the roof structure is left sound. We survey first, and if we find a problem beneath the foam we will tell you before we start rather than after.

How long does it take?

A typical loft is a matter of days rather than weeks, depending on the type of foam, how much there is and the access. We give you a realistic window at survey stage, and if you have a completion date we will tell you plainly whether it is achievable.

Is it messy?

It is a dusty job, which is why property protection is its own step in our process. Sheeting and containment go in before anything is removed, and all waste leaves with us.

Do I get a certificate at the end?

Yes. Comprehensive documentation and certification is issued on completion — that is the part your lender, equity release firm or buyer's surveyor is actually waiting for, and for most customers it is the reason for the whole job.

Do you reinsulate the loft afterwards?

Yes. Felt membrane, Rockwool or Therma Wrap foil depending on the roof. It means you are not looking for a second contractor in the middle of a transaction.
